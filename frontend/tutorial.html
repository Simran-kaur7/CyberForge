06

Human approval gate

Before a response action is executed, CyberForge puts a human in the loop. The analyst can approve or reject the proposed action.

AI RECOMMENDATION
→

Safety boundary: investigation can be autonomous; destructive containment requires explicit human approval.
07

Containment follows approval

When the analyst approves the response, the containment workflow can proceed. A rejection stops that response path while preserving the investigation context.
